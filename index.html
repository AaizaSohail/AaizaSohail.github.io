About Me

I’m an Aerospace Engineering student with a strong interest in engineering design, aerospace technology, and innovation. As the first woman in my family to pursue engineering, I’m passionate about creating my own path in the aerospace industry and inspiring others to do the same. I enjoy applying mathematics, physics, and programming to practical engineering problems and developing solutions through hands-on projects.

My experience includes CAD design, 3D modelling and printing, Python, Arduino-based projects and engineering simulations. This portfolio showcases my academic work, technical projects and experiences as I continue developing my skills and exploring the engineering industry.

My Projects

CREO & 3D Modelling

I turn engineering concepts into physical prototypes. In this project, I used Creo CAD to design and refine an aircraft model and personalised name stand, then transformed the final designs into 3D-printed prototypes. This project strengthened my skills in CAD modelling, dimensional accuracy, design iteration and taking a concept from a digital model to a physical component.

Supersonic Wind Tunnel Engineering Analysis

Investigated supersonic airflow through engineering analysis and experimental methods, exploring aerodynamic behaviour and how wind-tunnel testing can support design evaluation, validation and engineering decision-making.

SolidWorks Piston Assembly & CAD Design

Designed and assembled a detailed piston mechanism in SolidWorks, modelling individual components and refining dimensions through multiple iterations. Developed practical skills in 3D CAD, assembly design, dimensional analysis and engineering problem-solving while turning individual parts into a functional mechanical assembly.

NASA GMAT

Used NASA’s General Mission Analysis Tool (GMAT) to model satellite trajectories and explore orbital mechanics. Set up spacecraft and orbital parameters, simulated satellite motion and analysed how changes in orbital conditions affected the resulting trajectory. This project developed practical experience in spacecraft modelling, mission analysis and using simulation software to apply theoretical engineering concepts.
